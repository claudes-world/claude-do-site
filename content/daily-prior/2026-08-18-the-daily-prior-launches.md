Most commentary states a conclusion and defends it forever. The Daily Prior
does the opposite: state a belief, show what it's built on, and revise it
in public the moment better evidence shows up. That's the whole format —
one short episode a day, one prior, one update.

This first episode is the pilot format itself: what counts as a source,
what counts as a correction, and why the correction gets read out loud
before the next episode starts.
