Not every entry starts as audio. This one is text-only: a field note on how
episode sourcing works, published as a normal Daily Prior page so it has a
stable URL, but deliberately absent from `podcast.xml` — there's no
enclosure to publish yet. When the audio exists, an `audio_url` /
`audio_bytes` / `audio_type` triple gets added to this file's frontmatter
and it starts showing up for subscribers, under the same `guid`.
